## Context (as published)

- All 13 WSL home matches at Stamford Bridge. Cup matches and, subject to qualification,
  UEFA Women's Champions League league-phase matches at AFC Wimbledon's Cherry Red Records
  Stadium; Champions League knockout matches at Stamford Bridge.
- Every match is Category A, B or C, confirmed when the match goes on sale.
- Three sales windows per match: early bird (usually one week), general sale, last chance
  (from about 48 hours before kick-off).
